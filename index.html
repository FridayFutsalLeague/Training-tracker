How to use SP Trainer

Quick guide

1Workout

Choose your training day and enter the reps you complete. Your previous workout weight is automatically filled in as a starting point. Tap the circle to mark a set complete, add a note if needed, then press Finish & Save.

2History

Review your saved sessions, including the weight and reps you recorded for each exercise.

3Progress

Select an exercise to see your best recorded weight and a simple graph of your recent progress.

4Program

Change exercise names, sets and rep targets, or add and remove exercises without rebuilding the app.

5PBs

Your current PB is the highest weight you have saved for that exercise. We can expand this later to include rep PBs and estimated strength PBs.

Exercise pictures

The small illustrations are quick visual reminders of each movement. For your knee rehabilitation exercises, continue to follow the technique and dosage given by your physio.
